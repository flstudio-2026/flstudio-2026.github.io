Editions

One installer. The license picks the tools.

The Windows and macOS download is the same FL Studio application. Your edition key decides whether audio recording and the larger plugin set are available. Compare rows, then read the full breakdown on Features.

Fruity Edition

MIDI and pattern workflow. No audio recording.

Step sequencer, piano roll, and playlist for instrument and MIDI work. Choose this only if you never need to record a microphone, guitar, or stem.

Producer Edition

The usual working license.

Audio recording, Edison, Kepler, Slicex, and Automation Clips. This is the edition most people mean when they say they produce in FL Studio.

Signature Bundle

Producer tools plus extras.

Gross Beat, NewTone, Harmless, and ten additional plugins on top of Producer Edition. Time-based effects and pitch editing without a second shopping list.

All Plugins Edition

117 plugins.

Kepler Exo, Harmor, Sakura, Luxeverb, Ogun, and the rest of the Image-Line set. One license instead of collecting plugins one by one.

Independent notes. Proprietary software.

fl-studio.net is not Image-Line Software. Download buttons on this site use placeholder targets until a file URL is attached. FL Studio is proprietary. There is no source dump, and this site will never call it that.

Compare file size to the published numbers: Windows 1.03 GB, macOS 1.10 GB. If an installer is far smaller, stop. Windows SmartScreen may warn on a fresh signature. That is a reputation delay, not proof of malware. Read the longer policy on Trust & Security.

Hardware floor: Windows 10/11, 4 GB RAM, 4 GB disk, Intel or AMD. macOS 10.15.7 or newer on Intel or Apple Silicon. ARM Windows is not supported.
